# Make APEX and MONTOC feel real

## Goal
Strengthen the website as a credible corporate presentation by showing APEX's people and grounding the MONTOC case in verified public brand information and products.

## Changes
- Update the MONTOC story with verified facts from its official website: founded in 2018, 20K+ satisfied customers, and 250+ products.
- Add a product gallery inside the MONTOC section using official product photography and accurate English product names.
- Link each featured product to its official MONTOC product page and clearly identify MONTOC as the image source.
- Add a dedicated **People & Culture** section for APEX, presenting the existing leadership roles, bilingual service talent, training, quality coaching, and human-centered culture.
- Keep all content in English and preserve the current blue-purple visual identity.
- Verify desktop and mobile presentation and interactive MONTOC tabs.

## Technical details
- Download selected public product images from MONTOC's official storefront into the project rather than hotlinking them.
- Use the existing homepage, semantic design tokens, icon system, and image loading patterns.
- Do not invent employee biographies, contact details, or business claims that are not in the academic project or MONTOC's official site.
