# Improve the ABM BPO corporate website

## Goal
Update the English website to faithfully present the expanded academic project, use MONTOC as the only corporate client, feature only Customer Service and Sales & Marketing, and restore the blue-and-purple brand identity.

## Changes
- Replace all LushGlow references with MONTOC and present its beauty tools, makeup, and skincare business as ABM's sole corporate client.
- Remove Collections as a service and campaign; retain relevant collections material only where the academic project’s overall financial context requires it.
- Restore a cohesive navy, electric blue, and purple palette across the logo, backgrounds, accents, buttons, charts, and interactive states.
- Correct the customer service and sales metrics from the document: 35% cost reduction, 80/20 SLA, 5:30 AHT, 80% FCR, 4.2/5 CSAT, 200 new customers, 12% conversion, and COP 250M sales.
- Add concise sections for the expanded project: market opportunity, 2026 SMART objectives, campaign operations and staffing, quality assurance, compliance, financial model, continuity plan, and leadership team.
- Improve navigation and section order so the longer content remains easy to scan on desktop and mobile.
- Keep the contact and PQR interactions, updating their MONTOC references and restored palette.
- Add page-specific English metadata and verify the final page visually on desktop and mobile.

## Technical details
- Keep the current single-page TanStack layout and reusable UI controls.
- Use semantic design tokens in the global stylesheet rather than hardcoded page colors.
- Use compact tabs and summaries for detailed academic information instead of placing the full 43-page report verbatim on screen.
